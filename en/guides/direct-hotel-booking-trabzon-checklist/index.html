Booking Pelit Park directly: the right room and a more personal stay

A direct reservation is useful because you can describe the stay you actually want. Many online searches start with the economy category, while a sea view, separate beds or two bedrooms may suit your trip better. Our team can explain those differences before you choose.

Published by Pelit Park Hotel · Updated September 12, 2026

In this guide

  1. Start with the room that fits your group
  2. More room for a personal conversation
  3. Put the real extras into the plan
  4. Arrange an arrival that works for you

Start with the room that fits your group

A couple seeking a sea view can consider Deluxe Double; two friends may prefer Twin; four or five relatives may need the family suite. The suite has two bedrooms and two bathrooms, with a panoramic view from one bedroom. Its fifth-floor position also means stairs after the lift's fourth-floor stop and a sloping ceiling. Direct conversation brings these useful details into the decision.

More room for a personal conversation

Booking directly lets you discuss dates, arrival and the room choices available with the people who will welcome you. Our team aims to offer practical flexibility where it can. The benefit is an agreed arrangement for your booking, rather than an automatic room upgrade, unconditional date changes or a promise to beat every platform price.

Put the real extras into the plan

An adult-suitable extra bed costs €10 per day and a baby cot €5 per day. Breakfast-inclusive and room-only rates are both available, and parking is complimentary. Mention any particular meal request in advance: the team will assess whether it can prepare it. This personal approach reflects a family-run hotel, while keeping special requests realistic.

Arrange an arrival that works for you

Reception operates 24 hours, with Turkish, English and Arabic spoken within the team and translation tools for other languages. Normal check-in is 14:00; from 09:00, a ready room can be provided early free of charge. Otherwise reception can hold your luggage. Use the official booking or WhatsApp buttons below to discuss the room and receive the agreed reservation details.

Useful facts at a glance

  • Personal help choosing the actual room category.
  • Flexibility discussed for your own reservation.
  • Extra bed €10/day; baby cot €5/day.
  • Free early access from 09:00 when the room is ready.

Common questions

Will booking directly guarantee a free upgrade?

No. The team helps you choose from available room options and can discuss suitable arrangements; a better room is not automatically included.

Can I discuss a special meal before arrival?

Yes. Send the request to the hotel, and the team will let you know whether it can accommodate it.
