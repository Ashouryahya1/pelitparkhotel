Trabzon Airport arrival: a nearby hotel with free early check-in

After a flight, the useful questions are simple: how far is the hotel, will someone welcome you, and what happens if you arrive early? Pelit Park is approximately five minutes from Trabzon Airport by car in usual conditions, and reception is staffed 24 hours.

Published by Pelit Park Hotel · Updated September 12, 2026

In this guide

  1. The short airport-to-hotel journey
  2. Free early check-in from 09:00 when available

The short airport-to-hotel journey

The airport is one of the reasons guests choose this part of Trabzon. Management's approximate driving estimate is five minutes, depending on traffic. Reception can call a taxi for your departure and help you communicate your destination. This is taxi assistance; a private airport transfer is not included automatically with the room. The airport's official transport page is linked below.

Free early check-in from 09:00 when available

Normal check-in is 14:00. If a room is available and ready, we offer early check-in from 09:00 without an extra fee. When a room is not yet ready, reception can keep your luggage while you go out. This gives you an option for the morning without treating early room access as guaranteed.

Late flights and the first night's reservation

Reception works around the clock, with Turkish, English and Arabic communication available within the team. Translation tools help with other languages. A very late flight does not mean the hotel closes, but the room must be booked for the night you intend to use. Arrival shortly after midnight is different from the normal afternoon check-in for that calendar date.

A gentle first morning and the return flight

The Turkish breakfast buffet runs from 08:00 to 11:00 in the room marked R in the lift, with sea and Boztepe views. Breakfast inclusion follows the booked rate. If you arrive before your room is ready, nearby markets or a Forum visit can fill the time. Checkout is at 12:00; allow airport preparation time in addition to the short road journey.

Useful facts at a glance

  • Airport: approximately five minutes by car, traffic-dependent.
  • Reception is open 24 hours.
  • Free early check-in from 09:00, subject to a ready room.
  • Normal check-in 14:00; checkout 12:00.

Common questions

Do I pay extra for check-in at 09:00?

No, when a room is available and ready. Early access is offered free of charge from 09:00.

What if my room is not ready when I arrive?

Reception can keep your bags so you can go out while the room is being prepared.
