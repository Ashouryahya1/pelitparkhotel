Sea-view rooms in Trabzon: Deluxe Double and the family suite

A sea view is worth describing precisely. At Pelit Park, couples can choose the Deluxe Double category, while a larger family can stay in the two-bedroom suite. These options have different layouts and access, and the suite's view belongs to one of its two bedrooms.

Published by Pelit Park Hotel · Updated September 12, 2026

In this guide

  1. Deluxe Double for a couple
  2. A panoramic bedroom in the family suite
  3. The view comes with an attic layout
  4. Sea and Boztepe views over breakfast

Deluxe Double for a couple

The Deluxe Double category combines a double bed with a sea view. It suits two guests who want the view without choosing a two-bedroom family arrangement. New comfortable beds and a 50-inch Smart TV are part of the room amenities. Standard Double and Twin describe other room choices; neither category carries a general sea-view promise.

A panoramic bedroom in the family suite

The suite has one double bedroom and one bedroom with two single beds, connected by a private corridor. Each has its own bathroom. One bedroom offers the panoramic sea view; the other does not have that same outlook. Four people can stay in the suite, or five with an extra bed charged at €10 per day.

The view comes with an attic layout

The family suite is on floor five under a sloping attic ceiling. The lift stops at floor four, so the final floor requires stairs. That access detail belongs in the room choice as much as the photograph of the sea. A guest unable to use stairs needs another arrangement; this suite does not offer a completely step-free route.

Sea and Boztepe views over breakfast

The view is not limited to sleeping rooms. The breakfast room, reached with the lift's R button, looks toward the sea and Boztepe. The Turkish buffet is served from 08:00 to 11:00, with cheeses, olives, jams, honey, hot selections, tea and coffee. Breakfast is available through breakfast-inclusive rates; room-only bookings are also offered.

Useful facts at a glance

  • Deluxe Double is a sea-view double category.
  • Only one of the suite's two bedrooms has the panoramic view.
  • Suite access includes stairs and a sloping attic ceiling.
  • The breakfast room overlooks the sea and Boztepe.

Common questions

Do both family-suite bedrooms face the sea?

No. The panoramic sea view is in one bedroom only.

Does a sea-view room necessarily have a balcony?

A balcony is a separate feature and is not promised for every room in these categories.
