Trabzon stays for work, KTÜ or Farabi visits: plan the practical details

A work visit, university appointment or hospital visit often follows a different rhythm from a holiday. Your priority may be a repeat journey, separate beds or changing dates. Choose the hotel around the practical needs of the stay and keep appointment arrangements separate from accommodation.

Published by Pelit Park Hotel · Updated September 12, 2026

In this guide

  1. Identify the exact building and entrance
  2. Choose a room for the people staying
  3. Make uncertain dates part of the booking decision
  4. Keep the stay easy to coordinate

Identify the exact building and entrance

Use the address in your meeting or appointment confirmation. A university or hospital name can cover more than one entrance or building. KTÜ Farabi publishes its location and contact information through its official website; confirm the correct destination there before planning the hotel journey.

Choose a room for the people staying

Two colleagues or companions may prefer a Twin, while a solo traveller may choose a Standard Double. Explain the full guest count and any specific movement or access needs before booking. Do not infer step-free access, medical facilities or special equipment from general hotel descriptions.

Make uncertain dates part of the booking decision

If your meeting schedule or visit length may change, compare the rate's amendment and cancellation conditions before payment. An extension depends on available rooms and the price at that time. Contact the hotel early when plans change, rather than assuming the same room remains free.

Keep the stay easy to coordinate

Pelit Park offers Twin and Double options, Wi-Fi, free parking and 24-hour reception. Ask about the details you need for a work invoice or arrival arrangement before your visit. For appointments, visiting hours and medical matters, contact the institution directly; the hotel is an accommodation provider.

Before you book

  • Confirm the exact appointment destination.
  • Choose beds for the actual group.
  • Read change and extension conditions.
  • Share arrival needs without unnecessary private medical details.

Common questions

Does a hotel stay include a hospital appointment?

No. Arrange appointments directly with the institution.

Can I extend the booking on the same terms?

Ask the hotel; an extension depends on current availability and the applicable rate.
