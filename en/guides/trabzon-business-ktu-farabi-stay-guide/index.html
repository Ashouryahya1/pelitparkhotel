Staying near KTÜ and Farabi: a quiet Trabzon base for your visit

A university application or a visit to a relative at Farabi can mean early starts, uncertain plans and time spent waiting. Our guests making these trips value a clean, quiet place to return to. Pelit Park provides ordinary hotel accommodation in the Forum, university and Farabi area, with practical help from reception.

Published by Pelit Park Hotel · Updated September 12, 2026

In this guide

  1. A room for the people making the trip
  2. A larger group and the suite's access
  3. Breakfast and the day's practical needs
  4. Arrival, communication and changing plans

A room for the people making the trip

Twin rooms offer two separate single beds for companions, relatives or colleagues; Standard Double has a double bed. New comfortable beds, private bathrooms, Wi-Fi and 50-inch Smart TVs make the room a place to rest between appointments. The hotel hosts people visiting the university or hospital without presenting accommodation as a medical service or an institutional package.

A larger group and the suite's access

For four guests, the family suite provides a double bedroom, a twin bedroom and two bathrooms, connected by a private corridor. An extra bed brings capacity to five at €10 per day. One bedroom has a panoramic sea view. The suite is on floor five under a sloping ceiling; the lift stops at floor four, so this choice requires stairs for the final floor.

Breakfast and the day's practical needs

The buffet runs from 08:00 to 11:00, with Turkish cold dishes, hot selections, tea and coffee. Its room is marked R in the lift. There are breakfast-inclusive and room-only rates. ŞOK and other nearby markets cover everyday shopping, and İlknur, Alper and Yağız pharmacies are close by. For evenings or closures, the official Ortahisar duty-pharmacy list below identifies that day's duty pharmacies.

Arrival, communication and changing plans

The 24-hour reception can call taxis and welcome late arrivals. Turkish, English and Arabic are spoken within the team; translation tools help with other languages. Free early access from 09:00 is possible when a room is ready, and bags can stay at reception otherwise. Direct contact also makes it easier to discuss an extension or a change, with availability and agreed booking terms determining the arrangement.

Useful facts at a glance

  • Twin rooms have two separate beds.
  • Breakfast 08:00–11:00; reception open 24 hours.
  • Suite access requires stairs above floor four.
  • Official Farabi contact and duty-pharmacy links below.

Common questions

Is there a special hospital or university package?

This guide describes ordinary hotel accommodation for visitors. Appointments and university applications remain with the relevant institution.

Which room suits two companions needing separate beds?

Twin offers two single beds. Larger groups can consider the family suite, taking its final stairs and sloping ceiling into account.
