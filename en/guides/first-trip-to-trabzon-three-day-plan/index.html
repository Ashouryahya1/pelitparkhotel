Three days in Trabzon: Meydan, Ganita and a relaxed local stay

For a first short stay, three manageable days can be more enjoyable than a long list of distant attractions. This plan uses Pelit Park as a base for the city, waterfront and Forum area, leaving time for meals and rest. It can be rearranged around your flight.

Published by Pelit Park Hotel · Updated September 12, 2026

In this guide

  2. Day two: breakfast, Meydan and the waterfront
  3. Choose a comfortable return, not a rushed evening

Day one: settle in and explore the neighbourhood

Leave luggage at reception if the room is not ready; free early room access starts at 09:00 when available. For essentials, ŞOK is approximately two minutes away on foot. Forum is about a 15-minute walk, with an uphill return. A nearby Maydonoz Döner meal gives you a simple first-day option without committing to another trip across the city.

Day two: breakfast, Meydan and the waterfront

Start with the 08:00–11:00 Turkish buffet if your rate includes breakfast. Then take a dolmuş marked Meydan from outside the hotel for time in the centre. Our team's döner recommendation there is Katık's Meydan branch. Ganita is the seafront option for a stroll and seafood; from the hotel, Moloz is the dolmuş direction used for that waterfront outing.

Usual cold choices include several cheeses, olives, jams, honey and labneh. Hot selections include fries, börek, mini pizzas, pastries and omelette, alongside tea and coffee; some dishes change from day to day.

Choose a comfortable return, not a rushed evening

For the return from Meydan, look for TRT Bostancı. Keep that name in Turkish on your phone, or ask reception to call a taxi when a direct trip suits you better. A quieter evening back at the hotel leaves time to rest on the new comfortable beds. Rooms also have a 50-inch Smart TV for a break between outings.

Day three: views over breakfast and departure

The breakfast room has views of Boztepe and the sea and is reached with the R lift button. Keep the last morning light if you have a flight: checkout is 12:00, and the drive to Trabzon Airport is approximately five minutes in usual conditions. That road estimate does not include airport formalities. If you have more time, the official Trabzon tourism guide below offers ideas for extending the trip.

Useful facts at a glance

  • Day one: neighbourhood essentials and Forum.
  • Day two: Meydan, local döner and Ganita.

Common questions

Do I need a rental car for this city plan?

No. It uses walkable neighbourhood stops, dolmuş routes and optional taxis.

Does this itinerary include distant day trips?

No. It is a relaxed introduction to the city and waterfront, leaving longer excursions for extra days.
