Your first Trabzon trip: a flexible three-day hotel-based plan

Three days in Trabzon can be enjoyable without becoming a race between landmarks. This sample plan keeps arrival, a main outing and a flexible final day separate. Adapt it to flight times, weather and your interests rather than treating it as a fixed tour timetable.

Published by Pelit Park Hotel · Updated September 12, 2026

In this guide

  1. Day one: settle in and explore at your own pace
  2. Day two: choose one main excursion
  3. Day three: match the plan to departure
  4. Choose a base for the whole itinerary

Day one: settle in and explore at your own pace

Check in according to your reservation, then choose a manageable city outing such as Meydan and nearby streets. Keep the first evening flexible if you have travelled overnight. Save longer plans for a day when the whole group is rested and can agree on the pace.

Day two: choose one main excursion

GoTürkiye highlights Uzungöl's natural setting and Sümela Monastery among Trabzon's attractions. Pick the outing that interests you most and check current access and transport with official information or your chosen operator. Do not assume distant stops all fit comfortably into one day.

Day three: match the plan to departure

Use the final day for a shorter city visit, a shopping stop or an unhurried breakfast, depending on your departure. Confirm checkout and transport first. If you want more time in the region, extending your stay can be more practical than squeezing a full outing between checkout and a flight.

Choose a base for the whole itinerary

List the journeys you will repeat and compare hotel location, parking and room layout. Pelit Park offers free parking, 24-hour reception and Deluxe, Twin and Standard rooms. Check whether breakfast is included in your selected rate and discuss any late arrival before booking.

Before you book

  • Keep arrival day lighter than a full sightseeing day.
  • Choose one main outing for the middle day.
  • Check current access and transport before departure.
  • Leave time for checkout and the airport journey.

Common questions

Is this a bookable tour programme?

No. It is a flexible planning example; transport and visits need their own arrangements.

Can I combine every famous attraction in three days?

Prioritise the places you care about and check routes rather than promising yourself an overloaded schedule.
