Near Forum Trabzon: walking, shopping and everyday essentials

Forum Trabzon is an easy outing from Pelit Park, but a useful neighbourhood guide should also explain the walk back and where to find everyday essentials. Here are the details our reception team gives guests who want to shop, pick up groceries or eat nearby.

Published by Pelit Park Hotel · Updated September 12, 2026

In this guide

  1. The Forum walk, including the return
  2. Groceries close to the hotel
  3. A nearby meal or dinner on the seafront
  4. Nearby pharmacies and out-of-hours help

The Forum walk, including the return

The walk from the hotel to Forum Trabzon takes approximately 15 minutes. The outward journey is comfortable; coming back involves an uphill stretch and can feel harder with bags or children. You can walk down and use a taxi for the return if that suits your group. By car, management estimates around two minutes in usual traffic conditions.

Groceries close to the hotel

There are three markets in the surrounding area. ŞOK is about a two-minute walk from the hotel, with usual hours reported by our team as 09:00–21:00. Neşe is the local option we point guests toward later in the evening and usually remains open until midnight. Holiday or branch hours may differ, but these names give you a useful starting point without a trip to the centre.

A nearby meal or dinner on the seafront

For a convenient nearby döner, our team recommends Maydonoz Döner. If you combine shopping with an evening in the centre, our Katık recommendation refers specifically to its Meydan branch. For seafood beside the water, we recommend Ganita. These are the reception team's local suggestions, not meal services included in your hotel reservation.

Nearby pharmacies and out-of-hours help

İlknur Eczanesi, Alper Eczanesi and Yağız Eczanesi are nearby pharmacies identified by our team. For a pharmacy outside normal opening hours, use the Trabzon Chamber of Pharmacists' current Ortahisar duty list linked below. It shows the pharmacies on duty for the relevant date, rather than relying on an old article to name a permanently open shop.

Useful facts at a glance

  • Forum: about 15 minutes walking, with an uphill return.
  • ŞOK: approximately two minutes on foot.
  • Neşe is the team's usual late-evening market suggestion.
  • An official Ortahisar duty-pharmacy link appears below.

Common questions

Is walking back from Forum as easy as walking there?

The return is uphill and more demanding, especially with shopping or a pushchair; a taxi is another option.

Where can I find a pharmacy at night?

Use the official Ortahisar duty-pharmacy list linked in this guide for that day's pharmacy and contact details.
