A Trabzon hotel with free parking: driving from Pelit Park

Having a car in Trabzon is convenient, but parking in the busiest central streets can become the least relaxing part of the day. Pelit Park provides complimentary parking and a quieter base, so you can choose when to drive and when to leave the car behind.

Published by Pelit Park Hotel · Updated September 12, 2026

In this guide

  1. How our parking works
  2. Short drives for arrival, shopping and departure
  3. Leave the car for a Meydan evening
  4. Arriving before the room is ready

How our parking works

Hotel guests use the parking area free of charge. When it fills up, reception can coordinate an arrangement in the access lane, with the vehicle key left at reception so cars can be moved when needed. Speak to the team on arrival for this arrangement. It is a practical overflow solution, rather than an individually reserved space or an unlimited parking promise.

Short drives for arrival, shopping and departure

Our usual estimates are approximately 5 minutes by car to Trabzon Airport, 3 minutes to the bus terminal and 2 minutes to Forum Trabzon. Meydan is approximately 10 minutes away. Traffic changes these times, so they describe the location rather than a guaranteed journey. The combination is useful when a rental car is part of your holiday.

Leave the car for a Meydan evening

A central outing does not require taking the car into Meydan's traffic and finding another parking space. Dolmuş passes the hotel: take the Meydan direction into town and TRT Bostancı for the return. The Moloz direction is useful for the Ganita waterfront. Reception can call a taxi if you prefer to return directly after dinner.

Arriving before the room is ready

Standard check-in begins at 14:00, but free early check-in is possible from 09:00 when a room is available. If it is not ready, leave your bags with reception and start the day without carrying them. Reception operates around the clock, so a late arrival has someone to welcome you. Midnight arrival still needs the appropriate night's room reservation.

Useful facts at a glance

  • Parking carries no additional hotel charge.
  • Reception coordinates overflow and keeps the vehicle key.
  • Meydan-bound dolmuş passes directly outside.
  • Check-in 14:00; free early access from 09:00 when available.

Common questions

What happens when the main parking area is full?

Reception can arrange parking in the access lane and will need the vehicle key to coordinate moving cars.

Can we visit the centre while leaving the car at the hotel?

Yes. Use the Meydan dolmuş from outside the hotel; for the return, look for TRT Bostancı.
