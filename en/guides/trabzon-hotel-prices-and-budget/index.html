Trabzon hotel costs: rooms, breakfast and the extras families pay

A useful hotel budget starts with real inclusions and known extras. At Pelit Park, parking is free, breakfast can be included or excluded from the room rate, and the charges for an additional bed or baby cot are clear. This makes it easier to budget beyond the first price in a search result.

Published by Pelit Park Hotel · Updated September 12, 2026

In this guide

  1. What the room price buys
  2. Known extras, with a three-night example
  3. Breakfast and parking in your budget
  4. Choosing value instead of the smallest room price

What the room price buys

Standard Double has a double bed; Twin has two separate single beds; Deluxe Double is the sea-view double category. The family suite is a separate two-bedroom option. All rooms have new comfortable beds, a 50-inch Smart TV and a private bathroom. An economy room and a sea-view room represent different stays, so a low headline price does not describe every room in the hotel.

Known extras, with a three-night example

An extra bed suitable for an adult costs €10 per day. A baby cot costs €5 per day. Over three charged days, one extra bed adds €30 to the room total, while one cot adds €15. These are separate examples: they do not establish that both fit into the same room. Regular rooms accommodate two guests, or three with the appropriate additional sleeping arrangement.

Breakfast and parking in your budget

The Turkish open buffet runs from 08:00 to 11:00 in the breakfast room reached using the R lift button. It includes cold dishes, hot selections, tea and coffee, with views of the sea and Boztepe. Some rates include breakfast and some are room-only; use the inclusion shown on your selected offer. Hotel parking has no extra charge, which is useful for a driving holiday.

Choosing value instead of the smallest room price

Direct contact helps our team match the room to your group: separate beds, a Deluxe sea view or the family suite can matter more than the cheapest category. Direct reservations also allow a conversation about flexibility, with the agreed terms recorded in the booking. Nightly room prices vary by date and availability; the booking system gives the current room total rather than a permanent price in this guide.

Useful facts at a glance

  • Adult extra bed: €10 per day.
  • Baby cot: €5 per day.
  • Hotel parking: complimentary.
  • Breakfast: included only in the selected breakfast-inclusive rate.

Common questions

What does an extra bed cost for three days?

€30 for one extra bed at €10 per day, in addition to the booked room price.

Does direct booking automatically mean a lower price?

Not always. Its practical benefit is personal help choosing a suitable room and discussing the arrangements available for your stay.
