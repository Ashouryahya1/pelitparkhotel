About us

Your Perfect Getaway Awaits!

Founded in 2022, Pelit Park Hotel is ideally located just 5 minutes from the airport and city center, and only 2 minutes from Forum Mall. Minibuses pass right in front of the hotel, providing easy access to the city center. We offer clean and comfortable rooms designed for your relaxation, with free parking and friendly service. Our reception staff speak English and are always ready to assist you, ensuring a smooth and pleasant stay.
