The family suite: two bedrooms, two bathrooms

Two bedrooms are connected by their own private corridor. One has a double bed and the other has two separate single beds. Each bedroom has its own bathroom. The suite sleeps 4 guests, or 5 with an extra bed.

The panoramic sea view is available from one of the two bedrooms only.

Floor and access

The suite is on floor 5 with a sloping attic ceiling. The lift reaches floor 4; the final floor is accessed by stairs. Choose this option if everyone in your group can manage that last flight of stairs.

Arrival and departure

Check-in is 14:00 and checkout is 12:00. Early check-in is free from 09:00 when a room is available and ready. Otherwise, leave your luggage with reception. Reception is open 24 hours.

Trabzon Airport arrival: a nearby hotel with free early check-in

Turkish breakfast with a view

The open buffet runs from 08:00 to 11:00 in the room reached using the R lift button, with sea and Boztepe views. Enjoy cheeses, olives, jam, honey, labneh, hot selections, tea and coffee. Breakfast is included only with a breakfast-inclusive rate.
