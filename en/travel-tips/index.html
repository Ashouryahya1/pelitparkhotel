TRAVEL TIPS & GUIDES

Make the Most of Your Trabzon Stay

Arriving in Trabzon is effortless with Pelit Park Hotel as your base. Use these practical tips to plan, explore, and unwind like a local.

Essential Tips for Every Guest

Simple reminders to make your trip smooth—from airport pickups to planning day trips along the Black Sea.

Plan your airport transfer

Pelit Park Hotel is just 5 minutes from Trabzon Airport. Book a taxi in advance or ask our front desk to arrange a pickup when you land.

Pack for changing weather

Coastal breezes and mountain air can shift quickly. Bring light layers and a rain jacket so you stay comfortable on every outing.

Use nearby transport

Minibuses pass directly in front of the hotel toward the city center. For longer trips, we can help you book private cars or guided tours.

Stay connected

Grab a local SIM card at the airport or rely on our fast Wi‑Fi throughout the hotel—perfect for maps, messaging, and bookings.

Respect local customs

Modest dress is appreciated at mosques and cultural sites. A simple scarf or light jacket is useful when visiting historic landmarks.

Time your day trips

Start early for Sumela Monastery or Uzungöl to avoid traffic. Evenings are ideal for tea at Boztepe Hill or a stroll along Ganita Coast.

Guides Tailored to Your Stay

Our team loves sharing Trabzon. Use these quick notes to plan with confidence and experience the city like a local.

  • Ask reception to schedule taxis, day tours, or airport pickups—available 24/7 with multilingual support.
  • Keep a mix of cash and cards. Most cafés accept cards, but smaller markets and dolmuş rides prefer cash.
  • Prayer times influence opening hours at some sites. Check schedules for mosques and museums before heading out.
  • Try Trabzon specialties like kuymak and pide near the bazaar, then head to the coast for sunset views.

Need personalized recommendations?

Our consulting team can map your day, reserve restaurants, and share the latest events across Trabzon.
