ARRIVAL PLANNING

Trabzon Airport Transport Options

Review official transport information and a live route to the hotel without a promise of a hotel-operated transfer.

Verify Transport on the Day You Travel

DHMİ lists HAVAŞ, municipal buses, minibuses, commercial taxis, rental cars and private cars among the airport transport options. Timetables and routes can change, so check the official source on your travel day.

Arrival checklist

  • Check DHMİ's current transport page
  • Open the live route between the airport and hotel
  • For a late arrival, share your flight and estimated time with the hotel
  • Confirm room, rate and late check-in conditions for your reservation
