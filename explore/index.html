Explore Trabzon

Discover Iconic Destinations Near Pelit Park Hotel

From historic monasteries to serene lakes and lively bazaars, these must-see spots put you moments away from the best of Trabzon.

Top 10 Places to Visit

Start with the legendary Sumela Monastery, then follow the coastline and the mountains to experience Trabzon like a local.

1

Sumela Monastery

A cliffside Orthodox monastery hidden in Altındere National Park, offering dramatic views and centuries of history.

2

Uzungöl

A postcard-perfect lake surrounded by lush hills, ideal for nature walks, fresh air, and scenic cafés.

3

Hagia Sophia Mosque

A restored Byzantine-era church-mosque with striking frescoes and sweeping Black Sea views from the gardens.

4

Boztepe Hill

A hillside lookout where you can sip Turkish tea while watching the city and shoreline from above.

5

Atatürk Mansion

An elegant villa showcasing early 20th-century architecture, lush gardens, and memorabilia from Atatürk’s visits.

6

Sera Lake

A calm reservoir west of the city, perfect for paddleboats, lakeside meals, and golden-hour photos.

7

Çal Cave

One of the world’s longest caves with underground streams, cool air, and wooden walkways for easy exploring.

8

Ortahisar Bazaar

A lively historic market where you can browse local spices, copperwork, textiles, and sweet baklava stalls.

9

Trabzon Castle Walls

Ancient fortifications that line the old town, offering a walking route through layers of Roman and Ottoman history.

10

Zagnos Valley Park

A green valley park with ponds, bridges, and cafés—perfect for an evening stroll close to the city center.
